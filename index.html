Questions

A few things couples ask.

Do you take the photos?+

Every image comes from a guest's own phone, often a photo taken just moments earlier. We handle the printing, cutting and pressing.

Are you insured?+

Yes — Magnetic Moments carries full liability insurance, and we're happy to provide a certificate of insurance (COI) for your venue upon request.

How does my guest get their magnet?+

Once pressed, it's placed on the gallery display for guests to find and take home before they leave.

How many magnets does each guest get?+

One is included per guest in every package. A second-per-guest option is available for $5 per guest.

How long are you there for?+

Roughly four hours of live production, timed around your reception. Exact timing depends on your package and event logistics.

How much space do you need?+

Sized to the footprint of a 6-foot table — enough presence to feel like a destination within your reception, without ever crowding the room. We'll work with your planner to place it exactly where it belongs.

What do you need from the venue to set up?+

A standard power outlet within reach, and Wi-Fi nearby to process photos. Let us know what's available at your venue so we can plan ahead.

Do guests need to download anything?+

Guests scan the QR code with their phone, then choose a photo they already have saved.

Does the price include travel?+

Yes — standard travel is included anywhere in our core Front Range service area (Denver, Boulder, Fort Collins, Colorado Springs, and similar). Mountain and destination venues (Winter Park, Vail, Aspen, Estes Park, and similar) are quoted individually.

How does payment work?+

A 50% retainer secures your date at booking, with the remaining balance due in full no later than 14 days before your event — leaving our full attention free for your guests and your celebration on the day itself.

What's your cancellation policy?+

Your retainer secures your date and isn't refundable. Cancel more than 60 days out and nothing further is owed. Closer than that, the full balance may still apply, since it's rarely possible to rebook that date.

How big is the magnet?+

Each keepsake magnet measures 2×3 inches — small enough to tuck in a bag that night, sized to actually earn a spot on the fridge.

Do you only do weddings?+

Weddings are our focus, but the same live magnet experience works well for corporate events, holiday parties, and conferences. Reach out with your event details and we'll let you know how it can fit.
